# Role and Responsibilities

**Title:** Founder & Financial Data Analyst

Primary responsibilities include:
- Analyze financial and operational data using Excel, Python, SQL, and related tools.
- Build financial models, forecasts, and scenario analyses.
- Develop KPI reporting, dashboards, and analytical templates.
- Perform data cleaning, reconciliation, validation, and quality checks.
- Conduct FinTech, financial, and industry research.
- Develop risk analytics and monitoring frameworks.
- Evaluate finance-related workflows and identify opportunities to improve efficiency and data integrity.
- Develop project scopes, analytical templates, and client-facing materials.
- Conduct prospect research and business-development outreach.
