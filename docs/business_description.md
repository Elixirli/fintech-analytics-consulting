# Business Description

The business provides financial technology and financial data analytics services to startups, small businesses, and other organizations that need support with financial analysis, modeling, reporting, and data-driven decision-making.

Services may include financial data analysis, financial modeling and forecasting, KPI and performance analysis, risk analytics, dashboard development, data validation and reconciliation, FinTech research, and financial workflow analysis.

The business applies tools including Excel, Python, SQL, and data visualization platforms to support financial analysis and improve the accuracy and efficiency of finance-related workflows.

The business does not provide personalized investment advice, manage client assets, or make securities buy/sell recommendations.
